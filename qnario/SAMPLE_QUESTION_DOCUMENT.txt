## JEE Physics - Questions with Equations & Diagrams

### Question 1
**Topic:** Kinematics

A ball is thrown vertically upward with velocity u = 20 m/s. 
The motion follows: v² = u² - 2gs

Find the maximum height reached. (Take g = 10 m/s²)

**[INSERT DIAGRAM HERE: Vertical arrow showing ball trajectory]**

**Options:**
a) 10 m
b) 15 m
c) 20 m *
d) 25 m

**Explanation:** Using v² = u² - 2gs where v = 0 at max height
0 = 400 - 2(10)s
s = 20 m

### Question 2
**Topic:** Circular Motion

A car takes a turn of radius r = 50m at velocity v = 10 m/s.
The centripetal acceleration is given by: a = v²/r

What is the centripetal acceleration?

**[INSERT DIAGRAM HERE: Top view of circular path]**

**Options:**
a) 5 m/s²
b) 2 m/s² *
c) 1 m/s²
d) 10 m/s²

**Solution:** a = v²/r = (10)²/50 = 100/50 = 2 m/s²

────────────────────────────────────────

### Question 3
**Topic:** Waves

The frequency of a wave is f = 50 Hz and wavelength λ = 6 m.
Using: v = f × λ

Calculate the wave velocity.

**Options:**
a) 150 m/s
b) 200 m/s
c) 300 m/s *
d) 250 m/s

**Calculation:** v = 50 × 6 = 300 m/s

## Formatting Tips for Your Document

### For Equations
✅ **Good:**
- Use Microsoft Word's Equation Editor (Insert → Equation)
- Type: x² + 5x + 6 = 0
- Result: Properly formatted equation

❌ **Avoid:**
- Plain text: "x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0"
- External images of equations
- Copy-paste from PDFs

### For Diagrams
✅ **Good:**
- Insert images directly (Insert → Pictures)
- Use: JPG, PNG, GIF formats
- Clear, labeled diagrams
- Reasonable size (fits in document)

❌ **Avoid:**
- Hyperlinks to images
- Very large files (> 5MB per image)
- Blurry or unclear diagrams
- Too many decorations

### For Text Formatting
✅ **Good:**
- **Bold** for important terms
- *Italics* for variables
- Normal text for explanations
- Bullet points for options

❌ **Avoid:**
- Too many colors
- Complex formatting
- Special fonts
- Excessive highlighting

### For Options
✅ **Format:**
```
**Options:**
a) First option
b) Second option *
c) Third option
d) Fourth option
```

The asterisk (*) marks the correct answer.

## Step-by-Step: Create Your First Document

### 1. Open Word
   - New blank document
   - Or use Google Docs (File → Download as .docx)

### 2. Write Your Question
   ```
   Topic: [Your subject]
   
   Question text with clear wording...
   
   [Insert equation using Word Equation Editor]
   [Insert diagram/image]
   
   Options:
   a) Option 1
   b) Option 2 *
   c) Option 3
   d) Option 4
   ```

### 3. Add Multiple Questions
   - Separate each with a horizontal line
   - Maintain consistent formatting
   - Keep questions clear and unambiguous

### 4. Add Equations (Word)
   - Click: Insert → Equation
   - Type your equation: x^2+5x+6
   - Automatically formats nicely

### 5. Add Diagrams
   - Click: Insert → Pictures
   - Choose your image
   - Resize to fit page
   - Add labels/captions

### 6. Save as .docx
   - File → Save As
   - Format: Word Document (.docx)
   - Name: "JEE_Physics_Questions_Easy.docx"
   - Location: Desktop (easy to find)

### 7. Upload to Qnario
   - Login as teacher
   - Go to: Upload Questions page
   - Select exam type: JEE
   - Select difficulty: Easy
   - Choose your file
   - Click: Upload & Parse

### 8. Verify
   - See preview of parsed questions
   - Redirects to dashboard
   - Questions available for exam generation

## Real-World Example: Complete Question

```
QUESTION: Electromagnetism

Topic: Magnetic Field

A straight wire carries current I = 5 A.
The magnetic field at distance r = 0.1 m from wire is given by:

B = (μ₀ × I) / (2π × r)

where μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A

[DIAGRAM: Concentric circles showing magnetic field lines
around a wire carrying current]

What is the magnetic field strength?

OPTIONS:
a) 10⁻⁵ T *
b) 10⁻⁴ T
c) 10⁻⁶ T
d) 10⁻³ T

SOLUTION:
B = (4π × 10⁻⁷ × 5) / (2π × 0.1)
B = (20π × 10⁻⁷) / (2π × 0.1)
B = 10 × 10⁻⁶ = 10⁻⁵ T
```

When this is uploaded and presented in an exam, students will see:
- Properly formatted equation
- Clear diagram with field lines
- Well-organized options
- Easy to understand and answer

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Saving as PDF instead of DOCX**
   ❌ Won't work
   ✅ Save as: .docx format

2. **Equations typed as plain text**
   ❌ "2x + 5y = 10"
   ✅ Use Equation Editor: 2x + 5y = 10

3. **Forgetting to mark correct answer**
   ❌ Answer not marked
   ✅ Add * after correct option

4. **Images linked from internet**
   ❌ Links break, images won't show
   ✅ Embed images directly

5. **Too much formatting/colors**
   ❌ Confusing, doesn't convert well
   ✅ Keep it simple and clean
